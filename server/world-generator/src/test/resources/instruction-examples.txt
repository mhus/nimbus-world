    - shape: CIRCLE
    - size: MEDIUM
    - positioned at the origin
  2. A large swamp region called "Whispering Marsh"
    - featureType: swamp-biome
    - biomeType: SWAMP
    - shape: CIRCLE
    - size: LARGE
    - positioned SOUTH of "Stoneveil Mountains"
    - distance: 1–3

    - featureType: island-biome
    - biomeType: ISLAND
    - shape: CIRCLE
    - size: MEDIUM

    - biomeType: FOREST
    - density: DENSE
    - shape: CIRCLE
    - size: MEDIUM
    - positioned WEST of "Whispering Marsh"
    - distance: 5–10
  5. A river called "Duskwater"

        - slots: SMALL
      - places: a trail connection point ("main-path"), a tavern ("marsh-tavern"), a garden ("village-green")
